# Defra Border Target Operating Model (BTOM) SPS Risk Tiers

## [BTOM-HIGH-01] High SPS Risk Category — Live Animals, Germinal Products & High-Risk Plants
Source: Defra Border Target Operating Model (BTOM) Statutory Guidance
Risk Scope: High

Under the Defra Border Target Operating Model (BTOM), High-risk SPS consignments including live animals, germinal products, and high-risk plants for planting (such as seed potatoes HS 07011000 and regulated nursery stock HS 06029099) require mandatory IPAFFS pre-notification at least 24 hours prior to arrival, an Export Health Certificate (EHC) or Phytosanitary Certificate, and 100% documentary, identity, and physical inspection at a designated UK Border Control Post (BCP).

## [BTOM-MED-02] Medium SPS Risk Category — POAO, Beef, Poultry, Dairy & Salmon
Source: Defra Border Target Operating Model (BTOM) POAO Risk Tables
Risk Scope: Medium

Medium-risk Products of Animal Origin (POAO) under BTOM—including fresh or chilled boneless beef (HS 02013000), poultry meat cuts (HS 02071410), Atlantic salmon (HS 03021400), and unpasteurised or chilled dairy cheese (HS 04069099)—require an official Export Health Certificate (EHC), IPAFFS CHED-P pre-notification before Great Britain port arrival, and risk-based identity and physical checks (1% to 30% depending on commodity and origin).

## [BTOM-LOW-03] Low SPS Risk Category — Grains, Oilseeds & Low-Risk Produce
Source: Defra BTOM Plant & Commodity Risk Matrix
Risk Scope: Low

Low-risk BTOM commodities such as milling wheat (HS 10019900), oilseed rape (HS 12051090), and low-risk fruit and vegetables (e.g., fresh apples HS 08081080 from EU low-risk origins) do not require a routine Export Health Certificate or routine BCP physical inspection, though commercial invoices, FSA mycotoxin traceability, and intelligence-led surveillance checks remain active.
