# UK Plant Health & Phytosanitary Manual

## [PHYTO-05] Quarantine Pests, Soil Prohibitions & Ware Potatoes
Source: Defra / APHA UK Plant Health & Phytosanitary Manual
Risk Scope: High

The UK Phytosanitary Manual enforces strict quarantine controls against Xylella fastidiosa, Ralstonia solanacearum (brown rot), and Epitrix flea beetles on ware potatoes (HS 07019090) and fresh tomatoes (HS 07020000) from pest-endemic zones. Imports of soil and growing medium attached to plants from third countries are prohibited unless certified pest-free with an official Phytosanitary Certificate and inspected under CHED-PP.
