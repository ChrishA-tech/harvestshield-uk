# Food Standards Agency (FSA) Food Law — Allergens & Border Recalls

## [FSA-LAW-06] Allergen Labelling (Natasha's Law) & Mandatory Food Recalls
Source: Food Standards Agency (FSA) Food Law Code of Practice
Risk Scope: Medium

Under UK Food Standards Agency (FSA) Food Law, Retained Regulation (EC) 178/2002 Articles 14 and 19, and the Food Information (Amendment) Regulations 2019 (Natasha's Law), importers and food business operators must declare all 14 statutory allergens on prepacked for direct sale (PPDS) and wholesale consignments, maintain one-step-back/one-step-forward traceability, and immediately withdraw or recall unsafe or contaminated food (e.g., Salmonella, Listeria, mycotoxins, undeclared allergens) while notifying the FSA and Port Health Authority.
