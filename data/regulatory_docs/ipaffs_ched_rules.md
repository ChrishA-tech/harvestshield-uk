# IPAFFS Pre-Notification & CHED Health Certificate Rules

## [IPAFFS-04] IPAFFS Pre-Notification & CHED Documentary Workflow
Source: Defra & APHA IPAFFS Operational Manual
Risk Scope: Medium

Importers into Great Britain must submit a Common Health Entry Document via IPAFFS (CHED-A for live animals, CHED-P for POAO, CHED-PP for plants and plant products) using the exact 8-digit CN/HS commodity code and valid UK EORI number. Failure to pre-notify via IPAFFS or mismatched EHC/Phytosanitary Certificate serial numbers results in port holds, clearance delays, or statutory re-export and destruction notices.
